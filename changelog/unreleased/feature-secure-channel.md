### Added

- Secure channel, records: `SecureRecordStream` carries the bytes of a connection in AES-256-GCM records of 16 KiB at most, the nonce built from the IV of the direction and an implicit sequence number, the length as associated data, a close record of length 0; a changed, replayed, reordered, dropped or cut record, a byte after the close, or a record beyond the hard limit (2^24 per direction) closes the connection, the first record failing as `KeyConfirmationFailed`. Writes are serialized inside the stream.
- Secure channel, transport: `SecureTransportListener` and `SecureTransportConnector` run the handshake of the channel inside the TLS session of the connection, within the handshake places and timeout of the link server and the connect timeout of the link client; a refused handshake is logged with its reason (event ids 9051-9053, refusals paced by `LogPace`), nothing being sent to the peer. A removed key of the peer closes its connections with a close record first (`SecureConnectionRegistry`, event 9061), a handshake that ends after the removal included; the Very Back closes a connection 48 hours old (9062). Record failures are logged (9054-9055).
- Links, renewal: `ITransportConnection.IsRenewalDue` (2^23 records on a direction, or 24 hours, on the secure channel); the link client replaces such a connection by a new one, closes the old one once its requests ended (within the request timeout), keeps it when the new one cannot be opened and tries again after one minute (event ids 9008-9009).
- Back and Very Back: the "Handshake" settings, checked at startup with the keys (fail closed); the Back calls the Very Back over mutual TLS and the secure channel, the Very Back accepts the Back only through it. The development settings point to the signing keys of `.certificates/dev`, which `New-DevelopmentCertificates.ps1` creates with the authority tool, their password set in the user secrets of the two tiers, distinct from the one of the PFX.
- Tests: the first two records against values sealed by the Python package `cryptography`; round trips of random writes, every change of a record, the hard limit and the renewal threshold; over real mutual TLS, the done criterion of 0.6.0 (a tier with a valid certificate but a signing key not pinned is refused on both sides; a party in the middle that ends TLS with valid certificates of its own makes the signatures fail; one that holds the stolen certificates only relays records it cannot read), a server without the channel, a removed key closing an established connection, the age limit; the renewal by the link client (replaced, a request in flight answered, the old one kept on a failure); a Back and a Very Back without valid keys do not start.

### Changed

- Transport: a TLS connection keeps the certificate this end presented in its session, so that the secure channel hashes it (`SslStream.LocalCertificate` is null on a TLS 1.3 client).
- Test kit: `TestTlsFiles` also writes the signing keys of the Back and the Very Back, adds their "Handshake" settings, and gives the Back a connector of the Very Back over the secure channel.
- Docs: `doc/dev/protocol.md` (the layer of the secure channel, the heartbeat on authenticated records, the renewal, the event ids), `doc/dev/secure-channel.md` (the certificates taken from the session, the event ids), `doc/adm/certificates.md` (protecting the key files, setting up, rotating and revoking the signing keys, events 9051-9062), `back.md`, `very-back.md`, `configuration.md`, `tools/certificates/README.md`, the coding conventions.
