### Added

- Certificate authority of the platform (`src/BoardGamesPlatform.CertificateAuthority`): an offline command-line tool, run by hand on the machine that keeps the root key, which opens no port. `init` creates the root (ECDSA P-256, self-signed, allowed to sign end certificates only), `request` creates the key of a tier on its own machine with its request (PKCS#10), `sign` signs a request for a role, `install` joins the certificate and the key into the PFX the tier loads, after checking that it comes from the authority, and `list` shows the certificates issued, recorded in `issued.csv`. The key of a tier never leaves its machine; the authority takes only the public key from a request, checks its signature, refuses a request of another role or with another kind of key, and decides everything else: the name of the role, its usages, a validity of at most 397 days within the one of the root. Keys are written encrypted (PKCS#8 with AES-256, PFX with AES-256 and SHA-256), never over an existing file, readable by their owner only on Linux; passwords come from `BOARDGAMESPLATFORM_CA_PASSWORD` and `BOARDGAMESPLATFORM_CERTIFICATE_PASSWORD` or a hidden prompt, never from the command line.
- Socket protocol, identity of the tiers (`PlatformIdentity`, `PlatformRole`): the name a certificate carries for each role, `gateway`, `back` or `veryback` followed by the suffix of the deployment (for example `back.bgp.internal`), and its usages (TLS server for the Back and the Very Back, TLS client for the Gateway and the Back), shared by the authority and the tiers that check the certificates.
- Tests (`BoardGamesPlatform.CertificateAuthority.Tests`): what the root and the certificate of each role hold, the requests refused (tampered, another role, another kind of key, invalid validity, an authority without its key or that is not one), and the commands from the root to the PFX of a tier.
