### Fixed

- Protocol tests (`LinkRenewalTests`): the gated ping is in the gate before the test sends its other pings; one of them could reach the peer first, take the gate and wait until the timeout of the test; the list of the connections opened is read under a lock, and the gate is released even when a check fails.
- Protocol tests (`HeartbeatTests`): a large request read slowly gives the writer at most 250 ms after each read, instead of waiting for it without a limit; the probable cause of the timeouts (4 in 60 runs on Fedora, 19 in 40 under load): the system wakes a blocked writer only once enough of its buffer is free, so the writer could wait for the next read while the test waited for the writer.
- Protocol tests (`HeartbeatTests`): short requests sent to a silent server wait for their read together with the close of the client, which closes on a task of its own once the clock passes its timeout; the probable cause of the timeouts (2 on Fedora): a request sent while the client closes may never be written, and its read was waited for until the timeout of the test.
- Gateway tests (`PlatformHubBackLinkTests`): the disconnection of a front reaches the hub over a WebSocket of the test server, whose close ends the connection on the server at once, instead of long polling, whose stop the SignalR client waits for without a time limit (the suspected cause: the timeout was not reproduced); each step is bounded, so that a failure names it.
